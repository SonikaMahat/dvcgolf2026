Format Four-Ball Match Play. Each two-player cart competes against an opposing two-player cart. All four golfers play their own ball throughout the hole, and each cart's lowest net score counts as its score for that hole. The cart with the lower best-ball score wins the hole.
Scoring Win = 2, Tie = 1, Lose = 0.
Day 1 Handicap Procedure Four-Ball Match Play (90%).
For each four-player match, each golfer's Course Handicap will first be calculated for the tees being played. The golfer with the lowest Course Handicap in the foursome will play at 0 strokes. Each of the other three golfers will receive 90% of the difference between their Course Handicap and the lowest golfer's Course Handicap, rounded to the nearest whole stroke.

Strokes will be taken on the course's designated handicap holes, beginning with Stroke Index #1, then #2, #3, and so on.

Example: Course HCs of 8, 13, 18 and 23 play as 0, 5, 9 and 14 strokes, respectively.

Rules USGA Rules of Golf will govern all play except where specifically modified by the Tournament Local Rules. See the USGA rules →
Tournament Local Rule As a modification to USGA Rule 18, a lost ball may be dropped near the estimated point where the ball entered the area in which it was lost, no nearer the hole, with a one-stroke penalty. Players are not required to return to the location of the previous stroke.

Par-3 Lost Ball Local Rule: If a player loses a ball on a Par-3 tee shot, the player has to hit another tee shot with a one-stroke penalty, unless the course provides a drop zone for that particular Par-3 hole.

Pace of Play

To keep play moving and enjoyable for everyone:

  • Pick up when your hole is decided. If you can no longer win or tie the hole, pick up and move on.
  • Limit lost ball searches to 3 minutes.
  • Putt out unless your opponent explicitly concedes the putt.
  • Be ready to play. Check yardage, select your club and prepare while others are playing.
  • Use “drop and go.” When cart partners’ balls are apart, drop one player with the needed club(s), then proceed to the other ball.
  • Use ready golf when appropriate and agreed upon.
  • Keep up with the group ahead. If a full hole opens ahead of you, make every effort to close the gap.

Format Singles Match Play. Each DMV player competes head-to-head against one Charlotte player. Four golfers tee off together, with two separate singles matches in each foursome. Each golfer plays their own ball, with handicap strokes applied where applicable; the lower net score wins the hole. The player who wins more holes over 18 holes wins the match. A match that is all square after 18 holes is halved.
Scoring Win = 1, Tie = ½, Lose = 0.
Day 2 Handicap Procedure Singles Match Play (100%).
For each match, both golfers' Course Handicaps will first be calculated for the tees being played. The golfer with the lower Course Handicap will play at 0 strokes. The higher-handicap golfer will receive 100% of the difference between the two Course Handicaps, rounded to the nearest whole stroke.

Strokes will be taken on the course's designated handicap holes, beginning with Stroke Index #1, then #2, #3, and so on.

Example: Course Handicaps of 10 and 18 play as 0 and 8 strokes, respectively.

Golf Spirit

Golf is a game built on honesty, integrity, respect, and sportsmanship. Players are expected to compete fairly, apply the rules honestly, show courtesy to opponents and fellow players, care for the course, and respect the traditions of the game.

Compete hard, play fair, and enjoy the fellowship that makes golf and this tournament special.

      Weather & Wear

      Mid-September in the DMV typically brings cool mornings and warm afternoons, with daytime highs around the upper 70s to low 80s. Rain is always possible.

      Pack: Golf shirts, shorts/light pants, a light pullover or quarter-zip, rain jacket, golf shoes, hat and sunscreen.

      Dress Code: Proper golf attire and golf shoes are required. No denim/jeans, tank tops or metal spikes.
